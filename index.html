the story so far

engineer by trade,
teacher by heart.

Since 2022 I've been the Senior PHP / Full-Stack Developer at APN Phils. Inc. leading development of an enterprise CRM, architecting an Omnichannel VoIP system on SIP and WebRTC, designing a subscription-based SaaS marketplace, and spearheading the CodeIgniter-to-Laravel migration.

I designed the company's centralized OAuth-style authentication shared across enterprise products, built a real-time WebSocket gateway, and architected a multi-tenant Enterprise AI Assistant powered by RAG and LLM integration. In parallel, I teach C++ OOP, Python and Mobile App Development part-time at the National College of Science and Technology the same school where I earned my BS in Information Technology mentoring students through real-world software projects.
